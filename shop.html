Classical treatments. Individual care.

Every procedure has a specific purpose and suitability. Select a therapy to learn about the traditional approach, preparation and aftercare.

Classical cleansing procedure

Vamana

A classical therapeutic emesis procedure considered only for selected individuals after assessment, preparation and an informed discussion with an Ayurvedic practitioner.

Assessment & preparation

  • Review of health history, current medicines and suitability.
  • Preparation is prescribed individually by the practitioner.

Supervision & aftercare

  • Performed only in a suitable clinical setting under supervision.
  • Recovery, diet and follow-up guidance are tailored to the person.

This procedure is not suitable for everyone. A qualified practitioner must explain suitability, risks, alternatives and aftercare before any decision is made.

Ask about Vamana ↗

Physician-directed therapy

Virechana

A physician-directed purgation procedure in classical Ayurveda. Suitability, preparation and supervision are determined for each individual.

Assessment & preparation

  • Medical and Ayurvedic history reviewed before consideration.
  • Any preparation or prescribed substances are clinician-directed.

Supervision & aftercare

  • Observation and support are planned around the procedure.
  • Diet, rest and follow-up guidance are provided as indicated.

Never attempt this procedure or use purgative medicines without direct advice from a qualified practitioner.

Ask about Virechana ↗

Traditional medicated therapy

Basti

Traditional medicated enema therapies, with the type, substances and course selected by a qualified practitioner based on individual assessment.

Assessment & preparation

  • Suitability is reviewed in light of health history and current concerns.
  • The practitioner explains the selected approach before care begins.

Supervision & aftercare

  • Care is delivered by trained professionals in a clinical setting.
  • Course, recovery and follow-up are individualised.

Basti is a clinical procedure, not a self-care treatment. Do not attempt to administer it yourself.

Ask about Basti ↗

Traditional nasal-route procedure

Nasya

A traditional procedure using prescribed substances by the nasal route when considered appropriate by a qualified practitioner.

Assessment & preparation

  • Health history and individual suitability are reviewed first.
  • Substances and approach are selected by the practitioner.

Supervision & aftercare

  • The procedure is provided with appropriate clinical guidance.
  • Aftercare instructions are shared for the individual plan.

Do not put oils, medicines or other substances in the nose unless specifically advised by a qualified practitioner.

Ask about Nasya ↗

Specialised procedure

Raktamokshana

A specialised procedure in classical Ayurveda that is considered only after strict clinical evaluation and a clear discussion of individual suitability.

Assessment & preparation

  • Detailed clinical evaluation and risk review are essential.
  • Any required preparation is directed by the practitioner.

Supervision & aftercare

  • Only trained practitioners using appropriate safety protocols.
  • Monitoring and follow-up are planned for the individual.

This is not a general wellness service and is not appropriate for everyone. Clinical evaluation, trained practitioners and safety protocols are required.

Ask about Raktamokshana ↗

Traditional external therapy

Abhyanga

A traditional oil massage in which the oil, technique and duration are selected for the individual after a practitioner consultation.

Assessment & preparation

  • Discuss current health concerns, sensitivities and preferences.
  • Oil and approach are chosen for the individual.

Supervision & aftercare

  • Provided by a trained therapist under the appropriate care plan.
  • Home-care and skin-sensitivity guidance can be discussed.

Let your practitioner know about allergies, skin conditions, pregnancy or other relevant health circumstances before treatment.
